Kiran Kui, MPH

👋 Welcome 欢迎

I’m Kiran Kui — a public health data scientist passionate about turning data into impact.

I graduated from 🎓 Columbia University Mailman School of Public Health where I was trained in biostatistics, epidemiology, and machine learning.

Currently, I’m a Data Analyst at Tufts CTSI and leverage data-driven solutions to address real-world health challenges.

我是Kiran Kui，一名公共卫生数据科学家，期望通过数据推动社会进步。

我毕业于🎓哥伦比亚大学梅尔曼公共卫生学院，主修流行病学,生物统计学, 和人工智能。

目前，我在塔夫茨临床和转化科学研究所担任数据分析师，运用大数据解决现实中的健康问题。
