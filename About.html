About My Work 研究概述

💡 My Vision 研究目的

“My academic journey has been driven by a central question: why do some people develop disease while others, with the same genetic risks, remain healthy?”

✅ This question led me to study epigenetics as an undergraduate, where I learned how environmental factors—such as diet, stress, and pollution can activate or silence genes, profoundly shaping health outcomes. From this, I came to understand that health is determined not only by our biology but by the broader context we live in.

✅ This perspective motivated me to pursue graduate training in public health, where I expanded my focus beyond molecular mechanisms to the societal and policy-level factors that influence disease. I deepened my skills in data analysis, learning how to work with large, complex datasets—from air quality measurements to health records—to uncover patterns linking environment, behavior, and health.

✅ Through this work, I saw firsthand how rigorous analysis can generate insights that guide more effective policies and interventions. Whether measuring how pollution affects chronic disease or evaluating programs to improve community health, I found that data can be a powerful tool for driving change.

✅ Looking ahead, my vision is to integrate clinical, environment sensor, and behavioral data to build evidence that addresses the root causes of disease. I hope to contribute to solutions that make healthier choices easier and more accessible, using data to bridge the gap between scientific discovery and real-world impact.

我在本科阶段学习了表观遗传学，了解到很多疾病并非单纯由“坏基因”引起，而是基因与生活方式、环境相互作用的结果。饮食、压力、污染等因素会影响基因的激活，因此而决定健康。研究过程中我意识到，个体很难完全掌控所处的环境，而这些环境往往由政策和社会决定。这也促使我硕士修读公共卫生，学习如何通过数据分析推动政策改变，创造更健康的环境。未来，通过数据深入探索疾病与环境、行为之间的关系，并且推动公共卫生政策，带来更大范围、更深远、更持久的影响。
